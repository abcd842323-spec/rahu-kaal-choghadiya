Har Choghadiya ka matlab

Ye samay kaise banta hai?

Choghadiya: Sunrise se sunset tak ke samay ko 8 barabar hisson me baanta jata hai — har hissa lagbhag 1.5 ghante ka hota hai. Raat ke liye sunset se agle sunrise tak ko 8 hisson me baanta jata hai. Har din (weekday) ka pehla Choghadiya alag hota hai, isliye poori table roz badalti hai.

Rahu Kaal: Din ke 8 hisson me se ek hissa Rahu Kaal hota hai — Somvar ko 2nd, Shukravar ko 4th, Shaniwar ko 3rd, Ravivar ko 8th, Mangalvar ko 7th, Budhvar ko 5th aur Guruvar ko 6th hissa. Is samay naya kaam shuru karna varjit mana jata hai.

Abhijit Muhurat: Din ke beech (solar noon) ke aas-paas ka lagbhag 48 minute ka samay aam taur par shubh mana jata hai. Budhvar ko Abhijit varjit mana jata hai — is tool me Budhvar ko ye nahi dikhaya jata.

Note: Sunrise–sunset yahan astronomical calculation se nikala gaya hai. Aapke sthaniya panchang se 2–5 minute ka antar ho sakta hai. Vivah, griha-pravesh jaise bade shubh karyon ke liye sthaniya panchang / pandit se pushti zaroor karein.
